# TENDER DOCUMENT: GLOBAL INFRASTRUCTURE EXPANSION PROJECT 2026
**Tender ID:** T-2026-GIEP-001
**Issuing Authority:** Ministry of Urban Development

---

## SECTION 4: ELIGIBILITY AND QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### 4.1 Financial Standing
The bidder must demonstrate strong financial health to ensure the stability of the project.

**Criterion C001: Minimum Annual Turnover**
The bidder shall have an average annual turnover of at least **$50,000,000 (Fifty Million USD)** over the last three financial years (2023, 2024, 2025). 
*Evidence Required:* Audited Balance Sheets and Profit & Loss statements for the mentioned years.

**Criterion C002: Net Worth**
The bidder's net worth should be positive in each of the last three financial years.
*Evidence Required:* Certificate from a Chartered Accountant.

---

### 4.2 Technical Experience
Experience in similar large-scale infrastructure projects is essential for the successful delivery of this contract.

**Criterion C003: Project Experience**
The bidder must have successfully completed at least **three (3) infrastructure projects** of value exceeding **$20,000,000 each** within the last seven (7) years.
*Evidence Required:* Completion certificates from clients.

**Criterion C004: Key Personnel**
The Project Manager must have at least **15 years of experience** in civil engineering, with a minimum of 5 years specifically in urban infrastructure.
*Evidence Required:* Detailed CV and professional certifications.

---

### 4.3 Quality and Compliance
**Criterion C005: ISO Certification**
The bidder shall possess a valid **ISO 9001:2015** certification for Quality Management Systems.
*Evidence Required:* Copy of valid certificate.

**Criterion C006: Safety Record (Ambiguous)**
Bidders should ideally have a low incident rate on their previous projects. Preference will be given to those with zero fatalities in the last 24 months.
*Note:* This is a preferred criterion but mandatory reporting is required.

---

### 4.4 Mandatory Documents Checklist
Failure to submit any of the following will result in immediate disqualification:
1.  Letter of Intent.
2.  Bid Security Bond of $500,000.
3.  Signed Anti-Corruption Declaration.
4.  Power of Attorney for the authorized signatory.

---

## SECTION 5: SUBMISSION GUIDELINES
All documents must be uploaded in PDF format. Scanned copies must be clear and legible. Documents in languages other than English must be accompanied by a certified translation.
